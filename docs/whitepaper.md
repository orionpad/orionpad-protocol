# ORIONPAD Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Every token starts somewhere, and most of them lose track of where. ORIONPAD exists to keep a token's starting point in view: where it was released, by whom, and how the market has treated it since.

The mission is simple. Give teams on Robinhood Chain a launch desk they can rely on, and give everyone else a public board that shows plainly what is moving.

## Product

- **Launch desk.** A place for a team to define a token and put it out on Robinhood Chain.
- **Live board.** The busiest pools on the network, with price, daily change, volume and liquidity, refreshed continuously.
- **Pinned slot.** The first row of the board belongs to the ORIONPAD token itself.

## Roadmap

1. **Foundation.** Site, board and brand go public; the token slot stays reserved at the top.
2. **Release.** The ORIONPAD token goes out on Robinhood Chain and takes its pinned row.
3. **Desk.** The launch desk opens so other teams can put out their own tokens.
4. **Reach.** Wider tooling and partner integrations, shaped by what the first three stops teach.
